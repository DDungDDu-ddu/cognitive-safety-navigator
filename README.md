# Cognitive Safety Navigator

Cognitive Safety Navigator is a browser-based case management prototype designed to help users organize safety-related situations, classify risk levels, and connect problems to support resources across language and social barriers.

## Project Goal

The goal of this project is to explore how AI-inspired design and cognitive science can help people understand risk, reduce confusion, and find safer next steps when they face difficult situations.

## Why This Project Matters

Many people struggle to find help when they face safety-related problems. This can become harder when they face language barriers, information overload, or uncertainty about what step to take next.

This project focuses on organizing safety-related information in a simple and understandable way. It does not replace professionals, but it helps users think through their situation more clearly.

## Target Users

- International students
- Immigrants
- People with language barriers
- People who feel confused when seeking help
- People facing online or real-world safety concerns

## Main Features

- Add a safety-related case
- Choose a category
- Mark the risk level
- Track the case status
- Save notes
- View support resources
- See simple safety recommendations

## Categories

- Mental Health
- Housing
- School Issue
- Immigration
- Online Scam
- Discrimination
- Emergency

## Safety Notes

This project does not provide medical, legal, or emergency advice.

It is only a prototype for organizing information and identifying safer next steps. In urgent situations, users should contact emergency services or qualified professionals.
